I am Oetari Dwika (widely known as @uoetari and curator of the visual essay publication @soothutari). With a background spanning information architecture, statistical data analysis, and publication design, I dissect intricate systems—from environmental carbon flows to labor economics—and render them into visual forms that readers can feel, remember, and dissect.

Unlike generic dashboard automation, my studio treats data visualization as fine editorial literature. Every diagram is typographically calibrated, rhythmically structured, and layered with visual metaphors designed to cut through information overload.
